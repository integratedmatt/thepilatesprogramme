# What changed after your feedback

You asked for a smarter way than reading every page. This is it: only the lines that changed, old next to new, with the reason. Everything below came from your Mat and homepage video, applied across every page, with your old website copy as the starting point.

Where it says **Rule**, the same change has been made on every page it appeared on, and the build now refuses to publish if the old wording creeps back in.

## Rules applied everywhere

| Rule | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Certify, not qualify | "Qualify to teach...", "I want to qualify", "I'm already qualified", "After you qualify", "Not qualified yet?" | "Certify to teach...", "I want to certify", "I'm already a teacher", "After you certify", "Not certified yet?" |
| Hours listed separately | "80 supported practice hours", "around 80 hours of practice" | "30 observation, 25 personal study and 25 practice teaching hours, in your own time" |

| Exams | "The three exams", "Teach a 45-minute class plus 5 random exercises, via Zoom" | "The exams", "all online", "Programme and deliver a 45-minute class to show you can plan and teach in a real studio setting. Online, with a client you source." |

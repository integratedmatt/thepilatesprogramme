# What changed, page by page

You asked for a smarter way than reading every page. This is it: the lines that changed, old next to new, with the reason. It covers everything since your Mat and homepage video, so you only need this one document.

Your old website copy was the starting point everywhere. Your notes on the Mat page were applied to every page they touched. Where it says **Rule**, the same change has been made on every page it appeared on, and the build now refuses to publish if the old wording creeps back in.

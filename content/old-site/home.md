#### Revolutionising Pilates & Barre Education

#### What We Do

The Pilates Programme is an internationally recognised Pilates and Barre teacher training courses that set teachers up for success!

We host our courses both at our Training Centre in Altrincham, Manchester and in partnership with luxury boutique studios worldwide. We keep our course sizes small which enables us to provide an exceptional standard of coaching. We focus on your individual development needs, all whilst building a community and valuing connection.

Train with confidence. Teach with purpose. Build a career without limits.

#### What Makes Us Different

Our courses are steeped in the heritage of the original methods yet combined with modern knowledge of training principles and the science of movement to optimise results for clients. Whether you would prefer to teach Classically or through a more contemporary lens, you will have all the tools to do so!

#### What You Will Take Away

Our trainings are a one stop shop in how to feel confident bringing your new vocation to life! Our standard trainings have modules that cover programming, modifying for injures, prenatal & postpartum, inclusive language……oh and how to make that killer playlist!

We structure our courses so that there is lots of opportunity to practice teach and receive coaching to refine this skill. Our promise to you is a wealth of knowledge that will set you up for success in teaching across a multitude of teaching environments including 1:1, group classes and virtually!

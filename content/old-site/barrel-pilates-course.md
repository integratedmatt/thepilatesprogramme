### Barrels Teacher Training Course

We love how the Pilates Barrels feels for our spines. It is stretch goals unlocked, especially the supported spinal extension. Be careful not to overlook the Barrel thought, it can also really pack a punch! Remember, the abdominals are responsible for spinal movement so whilst there are some delicious stretches involved, there is also plenty of ab work to be done!

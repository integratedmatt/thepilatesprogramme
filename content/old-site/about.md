### Welcome to the future of UK Pilates and Barre education!

Situated in the historic market town of Altrincham in Manchester; we are a luxury purpose-built education centre designed to optimise your learning experience.

We are proud to be the first of our kind in the UK, raising the standard and leveling up education within the fitness industry.

Unlike in-house trainings that work around a studio timetable, we are fully dedicated and committed to your teacher training experience. Our training centre offers;

- Full Pilates apparatus & Barre fit-out across 2 floors

- Integrated screens for seminars & presentations

- Study & breakout spaces

- Kitchen facilities

- Easy access by train, tram, us and car

- Parking nearby

- Equipment hire for Personal Practice

- Studio hire for for Practice Teaching

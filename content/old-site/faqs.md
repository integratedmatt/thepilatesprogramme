## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Do you teach Classical or Contemporary Pilates?

We teach it all!

You will learn Contrology (what Joseph Pilates himself taught), Classical Pilates (what the first generation of teachers then adapted) and then finally Contemporary Pilates (a modern, evolving approach to Pilates that builds on the original system but incorporates newer insights from fields like physiotherapy, biomechanics, and sports science).

#### Do I have to complete my Mat training before my Reformer training?

No, we encourage you to train in the apparatus which you are most passionate about and have the most experience in! We structure our reformer courses to include the history of pilates, the basic principles, anatomy and all of the fundamentals that are commonly only covered in mat courses by other education providers. Therefore we are able to certify directly in reformer. Please note, unless you also complete a Mat certification you will not be qualified to teach Mat.

No, we believe that each piece of apparatus deserves your full love and attention! We separate our training into separate courses so that there is no compromise in the quality of your education. You will receive the highest level of knowledge and support for each piece of apparatus.

We offer a £1000 discount for any student who books Mat, Reformer or Barre at the same time. The total course cost for both is £3400 and this can be split across 6 interest free monthly installments.

#### Do you offer a combined course?

Our Mat, Reformer & Barre courses are designed to progress the Pilates enthusiast into the Pilates teacher and therefore you do not need any prior teaching experience.

Our Cadillac, Chair and Barrels courses require you to have an existing Reformer qualification.

#### How much experience do I need to book on to one of your Teacher Training courses?

Like any other job, the more experience you have in something, the easier you will find it.

We ask for a minimum of 50 hours experience attending classes in your chosen apparatus. 30 of these should be attending ‘Classical Pilates’ classes so that you have a sound understanding of the original exercises. These can be clocked up on the road to your intensive course dates.

Fundamentally, you are more likely to succeed at something you are passionate about and there is more chance work will feel like play!

If you don’t have an existing level 3 Mat or PT qualification you will need to sit our Human Movement course which will take you through the anatomy, biomechanics, biological & energy systems of the human body.

#### Can I use your qualification to teach abroad?

Absolutely!

Did you know, Pilates is actually legally an ungoverned industry? This means that there is no rule as to where you have to complete your training. This puts the responsibility on the student to find a training provider that delivers a high standard of education in a supportive learning environment (don’t worry, we’ve got you covered)

Our programme is fully insured and accredited through ITTAP by The Pilates Method Alliance (PMA). This is an internationally recognised body who are committed to maintaining a high standards in Pilates education.

Our Prenatal course is endorsed by CIMSPA.

All of our students undergo comprehensive assessment, practical training and continued education to meet these hours. We will provide you with a ‘letter of validation’ that includes our content criteria, our international alignment and insurance coverage.

#### Can I train with you if my existing qualification is from a different education provider?

Absolutely, we allow anyone who has an existing certification from another accredited school to continue their education with us.

We are passionate about removing barriers to education in hope of supporting you to be the best teacher you can be.
